## 1. Executive summary

The technical foundation is now sound (Phase 1). The site's problems are trust and duplication, not code.

1. **Testimonials are not real.** 1,101 testimonial cards appear on 367 pages. There are 91 distinct testimonials, and none of them has a source. 39 are generated by templates and reused on up to 175 pages, with the city name swapped in so the same "customer" appears to live in 36 different cities. One text ("Called on a Saturday morning when our AC stopped working...") is on all 175 Google Ads landing pages under 8 rotating names. This is the highest-risk issue on the site: it misleads customers, is the pattern the FTC rule on fake reviews (16 CFR Part 465) targets, and undermines every other trust signal.
2. **Trust claims are unsupported or inconsistent.** "100+ Google reviews" appears on 367 pages. The site holds 18 Google reviews, and a third-party directory lists the business at 5.0 from 9 reviews. "500+ Local Homeowners" has no source. "CSLB Certified" appears on 202 pages, but CSLB issues licenses, not certifications, and the license number appears nowhere on the site. The header says "24/7" on every page while the structured data says closed on Sundays.
3. **Four of the 18 Google reviews on the homepage are written in the business's own voice** ("Our expert technicians provide...", "Contact us for same-day AC repair"). They read as marketing copy, not customers.
4. **Most location pages are the same page with a different city name.** After removing city names, the generated city × service pages are 97 to 98% identical to their nearest sibling and have about 1% unique text. The 175 Ads pages are 100% identical. Only 10 hand-written pages (5 AC repair, 5 emergency AC repair) have meaningful unique content.
5. **Up to 7 URLs per city compete for the same searches.** For Yorba Linda, the homepage, 5 city × service pages, the service-area page and an Ads page all carry "Yorba Linda" plus "AC repair" or "HVAC" in their titles. No page targets "AC installation Yorba Linda".
6. **Location hubs and service pages do not link to each other.** None of the 21 Orange County city hub pages links to any of its 5 city service pages, and none of those pages links back.
7. **Performance is acceptable in the lab** (Lighthouse mobile 0.74 to 0.88, layout shift 0). The main cost is Google Tag Manager and Google Ads tags (about 400 to 500 ms of main-thread time on every page). Real-user data is unavailable.
8. **Competitors are beatable on substance.** The strongest local competitor on content (Dial One Sonshine) uses the same templated city pages; its Yorba Linda URL now leads to a *plumbing* page. None of the fetched competitor pages mention the 2025 R-454B refrigerant transition. The advantage available to this business is real, specific, local proof that templated competitors do not have.

**The single most valuable action** is to remove the generated testimonials and unsupported numbers and replace them with the 14 genuine-looking Google reviews already on file, a real review count and the CSLB license number. That needs owner input, not engineering effort.

### 3.5 Recommendations (no rewriting with new city names)

- **Do not "uniquify" generated pages by rewording.** Changing synonyms or sentence order across 300 pages produces the same thin pages with more effort.
- **One strong page per city, not seven.** The service-area city page is the only family with real per-city facts. Make it the city hub (section 6) and add owner-verified local substance: neighbourhoods actually served, typical housing stock and systems seen on real jobs, real response times from the shop, permit notes, real local jobs.
- **Keep city × service pages only where they have substance or traffic.** The 10 hand-written pages qualify. For generated pages, keep them only if GSC shows clicks; otherwise merge into the city hub with a 301 (MERGE CANDIDATE).
- **Ads pages:** keep live and unchanged for Google Ads; remove them from the sitemap and noindex them after a GSC check (NOINDEX CANDIDATE). Ads quality scores do not depend on indexing.

## 6. Internal linking recommendations

### 6.1 Findings (all 456 sitemap URLs)

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| City hubs and city service pages are disconnected | 21 Orange County hubs link to **0** of their city service pages; those 105 service pages link to their hub **0** times |

| Orphans (no links from any page) | 175 Ads pages (expected), 6 `/[city]-ac-repair` pages, 4 San Clemente city × service pages (no city lists San Clemente as "nearby") |

### 6.2 Strategy

1. **Hub and spoke per city.** On each `/service-areas/[county]/[city]` page add a "Services in {city}" block linking to that city's service pages with descriptive anchors ("AC repair in Yorba Linda", "Emergency AC repair in Yorba Linda"). On each city service page add one contextual link back: "More about our HVAC service in {city}". This is one template change for 21 to 37 hubs.
2. **Descriptive anchors.** Replace bare city anchors in "We Also Serve Nearby Cities" with service plus city ("AC repair in Placentia"). Fix the "Ac" casing at the source.
3. **Yorba Linda first.** Link the primary Yorba Linda pages (section 5.2) from: the homepage service cards, `/air-conditioning/ac-repair` (the generic AC repair hub, 57 links in), `/emergency-hvac-service`, and relevant blog posts.
4. **Trust pages.** Link `/reviews` from every testimonial section ("Read all reviews") and `/about-us` from the "Why choose us" sections. These pages carry the E-E-A-T signals.
5. **Do not add links to the 175 Ads pages.** They are not meant for organic search.
6. **Orphans:** add San Clemente to its neighbours' `nearbySlugs` (owner decides which cities are nearby); decide whether the 6 `/[city]-ac-repair` pages are Ads destinations or organic pages.

### 9.2 Findings

- **Third-party tags are the main JavaScript cost.** Google Tag Manager plus the Google Ads gtag (`AW-18204232188`) transfer about 316 KB and use about 400 to 500 ms of main-thread time on every page, more than the site's own JavaScript.
- **First-party JavaScript is reasonable:** 9 scripts, about 200 KB gzipped, mostly React and Next.js. Lighthouse reports about 160 KB unused on inner pages.
- **Contact page:** the Google Maps iframe adds about 450 KB and the LCP is 3.8 s.
- **Layout stability is excellent** (CLS 0 everywhere). All images use `next/image`.
- Homepage HTML is 424 KB uncompressed (53 KB gzipped) because of the inline React payload; acceptable.
- Repository hygiene: `public/uploads` (59 MB) includes two MP4s (3.3 MB and 2.6 MB) and `public/images` has 2 MB PNGs (`new hero image.png`, `hvac-hero-technician.png`, `app-icon-website.png`) that no code references. No effect on visitors; worth cleaning up.

### 9.3 Recommendations (no tracking changes without GTM review)

1. In GTM, list every tag and trigger; remove duplicates (for example a gtag config also fired from GTM); fire non-conversion tags on `window.load` or later. **Keep conversion tracking intact** and test it in GTM preview before publishing.
2. Replace the contact-page map iframe with a static map image or a click-to-load facade linking to the Google profile.
3. Re-measure with field data once available before doing anything more invasive.
